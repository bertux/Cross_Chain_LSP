Gas paid by the paymaster (experimental)

Your UP operates while the controller holds no gas: the controller signs, a relayer sends, your paymaster pays. ERC-4337 (EntryPoint v0.6).

0 · How it works

In plain words

Every time your UP does something on a blockchain, for example sending money, a small fee is paid: the gas. Normally whoever signs pays it, so the controller would need to keep a little money on every network. With this page it no longer does.

  • The cassa is the wallet that pays. It puts money in a piggy bank, the paymaster, and decides which UPs may use it.
  • The UP's controller only signs, the way you sign a form. It pays nothing.
  • The site relayer takes the signed form to the blockchain, advances the fee and gets it back from the piggy bank.

To prepare once:

  1. the cassa creates the piggy bank, fills it and sets the most it may pay for each operation (section 3);
  2. the cassa puts the UP on the list of those who may use it (section 4);
  3. the controller authorises the UP to use this system (section 4). It is the only time the controller pays a little gas.

Then, every time: the controller signs (A) and the site relayer sends (B). The piggy bank pays the fee; the UP pays only what it sends.

📘 Step-by-step guide: who does what, several MetaMask accounts, order of the steps

Who does what

  • Cassa, the paymaster owner: publishes, funds, sets the cap and withdraws (section 3), puts UPs on the allowlist or removes them (section 4). All of these are done with the cassa active in MetaMask.
  • The UP's controller: sets up the UP, once per chain (section 4), and signs every operation (button A). The setup costs a little gas; operations do not.
  • Site relayer: sends the signed operation (button B). No MetaMask account is needed.
  • UP extension (optional): only reads the UP address. It signs nothing here.

The MetaMask accounts

  1. Always connect the cassa first. Make the cassa active in MetaMask, then press Connect MetaMask. In the MetaMask window you can tick the controller too: both stay connected.
  2. The Owner field in section 3 must always be the cassa. If the site runs a relayer, the page fills it in and remembers it. If you see another address, correct it: otherwise the page computes another paymaster, which does not exist.
  3. The page always uses the account active in MetaMask, and only that one. To switch, open MetaMask and pick the other account from the menu at the top; if MetaMask says it is not connected to this site, use its Connect button. Then check the MetaMask row in section 1; if it does not update, press Connect MetaMask again. An operation already signed is kept.
  4. UP extension and MetaMask active together is normal: they do two different jobs.

Every time you open or reload the page

  1. Tick I have read and accept below: without it the buttons stay grey.
  2. Section 1: choose the network and enter the UP address.
  3. Section 2: the check runs by itself and says what is missing and where to do it.
  4. Section 3: check that the Owner is the cassa.

Once per chain, with the cassa active

  1. Publish the paymaster, if missing.
  2. Fund it.
  3. Set the cap per operation.
  4. Section 4: if Extension4337 shows not published, press Publish Extension4337 on this network. Here the cassa only pays the gas: the extension is a public contract, the same for everyone, and whoever publishes it gets no rights over any UP. Once per network.

Each button opens MetaMask to confirm a transaction of the cassa. The paymaster and Extension4337 are published through the standard contract factory 0x4e59b448…956C: MetaMask may show it with no name or odd characters, that is normal.

For each UP

  1. Enter the UP address in section 1.
  2. With the cassa active, section 4, Paymaster allowlist: press Add the UP to the allowlist. After a few seconds MetaMask opens and asks you to confirm a transaction: check that the account is the cassa and confirm. It costs very little gas, paid by the cassa. After the confirmation the box says The UP is on the allowlist.
  3. Switch to the UP's controller in MetaMask (the genesis key or the backup, never the cassa) and check section 1: the Active account's permissions row in section 4 must show the permissions, not "not a controller".
  4. Section 4, Setup: Prepare and simulate, read the plan, then Sign and send and confirm in MetaMask. One transaction, with a little gas paid by the controller (its account needs a little of the network's currency); the page verifies the result. The signing controller receives the 4337 permission: to sign operations with the other controller too, repeat the setup with it active.
  5. The UP must hold on this network the money it wants to send: the paymaster pays the gas, not the amount.

Every operation, with the controller active

  1. A · Prepare and sign: compare the fingerprint in MetaMask with the page's, then sign. No gas.
  2. B · Send: done by the site relayer. No account switch, no signature.

While you wait

  • After each click a box at the bottom shows the status. Before MetaMask opens the page reads the data and simulates: it can take a few seconds. Do not click again.
  • When you switch account or network in MetaMask, the box Updating the data appears too: the page reads everything again and the buttons stay grey for a few seconds. Wait until it goes away.
  • If MetaMask does not appear, open the extension: the request may be waiting there.

Checking the configuration

  • Section 2, Check the configuration: read-only, no signature, no wallet. It needs only the network, the UP address and the paymaster owner. Each item shows ✅, ⚠️ or ❌; the most important one is EntryPoint's permissions = 0x000500. Run it after any change to the UP and on every network. The server also repeats it every hour and emails the operator if something changes.

Going back

  • Section 4, Turning 4337 off on this UP: one controller transaction restores the UP as it was. Section 4, Remove from the allowlist, with the cassa.

2 · Paymaster: a contract of yours (contracts/UPPaymaster.sol), at the same address on every chain for the same owner, that pays gas only for the UPs on its allowlist and up to a cap per operation. You fund it with a plain transfer; only the owner manages allowlist, cap and withdrawals.
3 · UP: the cassa puts it on the paymaster allowlist; then one controller transaction, once per chain: it registers LUKSO's Extension4337, adds the EntryPoint as a controller (it can call and send value, not change data or permissions) and gives the controller the 4337 permission. The controller's other permissions stay identical.

⚠️ Read before using this page
  • Experimental tool, not audited. The paymaster and LUKSO's Extension4337 have not been reviewed by third parties. Use small amounts.
  • It changes the UP's configuration on this chain: the EntryPoint becomes a controller with call and value-transfer permissions. It only executes operations signed by a controller with the 4337 permission, within the signer's own permissions.
  • The paymaster deposit is spent by every operation of allowlisted UPs: add only UPs you trust and keep the deposit low.

  • This page never asks for private keys: you sign everything in MetaMask.

Read-only: no signature, no gas. It needs only the network and the UP address (section 1) and, for the paymaster, the owner (section 3); MetaMask is not needed. 3 · Paymaster (with the cassa)

Everything in this section is done with the cassa active in MetaMask. The paymaster's address depends only on the EntryPoint and the owner: same owner, same address on every chain. Choose the owner carefully: it can only be changed in two steps (proposal and acceptance). Anyone can publish it and fund it; only the owner manages allowlist, cap and withdrawals. The cap starts at 0: it pays nothing until you set it.
It must always be the cassa. All paymaster operations (publish, fund, cap, withdraw) and the allowlist are done with the cassa active in MetaMask. If the site runs a relayer, the field fills in by itself with the owner of its paymaster, and the page remembers it.
Not checked yet.

3b · Sponsor paymaster (with the cassa)

A second paymaster, separate from the one in section 3: it has no list. It pays only for the operations approved, one by one, by the site's sponsor service, which signs with its own key (the signer). The service decides which UPs and how many operations; the paymaster checks the signature and the cap per operation, which limits the damage even if the service's key were stolen. Same owner (the cassa), different address. Signer and cap start at zero: it pays nothing until you set both. Stop now removes the signer: no operation is paid any more. Nothing changes on the UP side: same setup as section 4.

4 · The UP

Paymaster allowlist · with the cassa

With the cassa active in MetaMask. Add the UP to the allowlist opens MetaMask, after a few seconds, to confirm a transaction of the cassa (it costs very little gas). After the confirmation the box below says The UP is on the allowlist. Only UPs on the allowlist can use the paymaster.
Not checked yet.

UP setup · with the controller

Two steps, with different accounts:
1 · Publish Extension4337, only if the box below says not published: once per network, then it serves every UP. Any account can sign it, for example the cassa: it only pays the gas and gets no rights over any UP, because the contract is public and the same for everyone. In MetaMask the transaction goes to the standard contract factory 0x4e59b448…956C, which may show no name or odd characters: that is normal.
2 · Set up the UP with the UP's controller active in MetaMask (the genesis key or the backup, never the cassa): one atomic Key Manager transaction, with a little gas paid by the controller; if any part fails, nothing changes. The signing controller receives the 4337 permission. Before signing you see the list of operations and the simulation.

Turning 4337 off on this UP

Undoes the setup with one transaction of the controller: it removes the extension for validateUserOp, the EntryPoint's permissions and its place in the controllers list, and the 4337 permission of the active account. Afterwards no operation can go through the EntryPoint on this UP. The other permissions stay identical. It can be set up again at any time.
